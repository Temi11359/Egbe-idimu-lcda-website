01 / ABOUT THE COUNCIL

A digital front door to local government.

Egbe-Idimu LCDA is a grassroots administrative area within Alimosho, Lagos State. The council serves communities including Egbe, Idimu and Isheri-Olofin, with public administration organized through departments and specialist units.

This portal is designed as a modern information experience: residents can quickly understand what each department does, identify the type of help it provides, and find the right route for further enquiries.
